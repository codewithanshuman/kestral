Halo lights

Twin ring lamps give the K1 a face you recognise from a block away, and light the road with a single clean beam.

Glass canopy

A single panel of glass runs from the windshield over the cabin. It filters 99% of UV and keeps the interior bright and cool.

Two-tone body

Gloss red over a pearl-white roof arch and pillars. Every panel is sculpted in the wind tunnel, so nothing is there for show.

Quiet cabin

Hand-stitched seats, one slim display and almost no buttons. Triple-laminated glass keeps road noise out at any speed.

Shaped by air, not by trend.

Every surface on the K1 was drawn in a wind tunnel first and a studio second. The result is a body with no vents, no handles and no wasted line.
